How It Works

ClearPath is designed to connect emergency vehicles, nearby drivers, and traffic signals automatically.

  1. An ambulance's siren activates its ClearPath signal.
  2. Nearby drivers receive an alert that an emergency vehicle is approaching.
  3. ClearPath requests priority at equipped intersections to help the ambulance move through.
